# SeeFirst Photography Brief
## 5-Shot Series: Visual Story of Trust

**Project:** SeeFirst (Trust-enabled e-commerce, Kumasi → Accra)  
**Shoot Date:** Week of [TBD]  
**Locations:** Kejetia Market (Kumasi), Accra residential area  
**Talent:** 1 MoMo agent (Kejetia), 1 seller, 1 buyer (Accra)  
**Duration:** 1–2 days  
**Deliverables:** High-res + mobile-cropped variants, all RAW files

---

## Visual Narrative Arc

**DOUBT → SEEING → SEALING → ARRIVING → TRUSTING**

Each shot builds the journey. Same seller, same item, same numbered seal across all shots. Continuity is critical.

---

## SHOT 1: Buyer at Home — Doubt
**Purpose:**  
Homepage hero, tracker waiting state, buyer request form context  
Shows the emotional starting point — fear of remote purchase

**Composition:**
- Accra residential setting (apartment, flat, modern home)
- Buyer (mid-20s to 40s, relatable) holding phone, scrolling
- Phone screen shows item listing (Instagram, Jiji, or text "₵2,500 phone")
- Face shows thoughtful concern, not panic
- Natural light from window, morning/midday
- Frame: sitting or standing, relaxed but attentive

**What's in the frame:**
- Buyer, phone, room (warm, lived-in, not sterile)
- Listing screenshot visible on phone OR close-up of phone edge showing WhatsApp
- Optional: Cup of tea, personal touches

**What's NOT shown:**
- Personal ID, address details, private information

**Continuity markers:**
- Buyer wears consistent clothing/jewellery across shots 1 and 5
- Phone is same device in shots 1, 2, 5

**Colour grading:**
- Warm, natural daylight
- Slight orange/amber cast (trust, home feeling)
- Shadows not too dark (approachable, not ominous)
- Contrast: medium (readable on mobile)

**Desktop crop (16:9):**
- Buyer centred, phone visible, room context

**Mobile crop (9:16):**
- Vertical: Buyer fills frame top to mid-chest, phone prominent
- Readable at 360px width

## SHOT 2: Agent Counter — Verification
**Purpose:**  
Tracker "agent checking" state, homepage "how it works" section  
Shows professional identity verification, neutral third party

**Composition:**
- Kejetia Market, inside a MoMo agent's stall or counter
- Agent (30s–50s, calm, professional) across counter from seller
- Seller holding Ghana Card (image blurred for privacy), or agent reviewing it
- Counter/desk visible with payment device, notepad
- Natural market lighting (overhead fan, window light)
- Frame: agent and seller in profile or 3/4 view

**What's in the frame:**
- Agent's counter/workspace (shows this is an official checkpoint)
- Ghana Card (must be blurred, not readable)
- Seller's hand holding card or item
- Market stall background (busy, authentic)

**What's NOT shown:**
- Ghana Card number, name, photo (privacy)
- Full seller face (if privacy concern)

**Continuity markers:**
- Same agent in shots 2, 3, 4
- Same seller in shots 1(implied), 2, 3, 4
- Agent wears consistent SeeFirst branded shirt/badge

**Colour grading:**
- Warm, daylit market (natural fluorescents ok)
- Slight green cast from outdoor market light is fine
- Professional but authentic (not studio-lit)

**Desktop crop (16:9):**
- Agent and seller across counter, workspace visible

**Mobile crop (9:16):**
- Vertical: Agent's hands and counter top
- Focus on Ghana Card (blurred) and professional setting

## SHOT 3: Hands Sealing Parcel — The Proof
**Purpose:**  
Tracker "sealed" state, homepage hero, key trust moment  
Shows the numbered seal being applied on camera, the core visual

**Composition:**
- Same counter as Shot 2 OR shipping prep area
- Agent's and seller's hands (or buyer's hand if remote) packing and sealing parcel
- Item partially visible in tissue/wrapping (don't expose what it is)
- Numbered void-pattern seal clearly visible and readable
- Seal being applied, or already sealed on package
- Close-up: seal number legible (e.g., SF-000015)
- Camera angle: overhead or angled to show seal placement
- Hands in frame show care and intention

**What's in the frame:**
- Parcel/box (neutral, white or kraft)
- Numbered seal (primary focus, MUST be sharp and readable)
- Tissue, padding (shows care in packing)
- Both hands (seller's + agent's, or seller + buyer on call)
- Optional: Phone showing buyer's face on live call in background

**What's NOT shown:**
- Contents of parcel (remains mystery for buyer)
- Any private identifying marks on package

**Continuity markers:**
- SAME NUMBERED SEAL across shots 3, 4, and 5
- Same parcel/box size
- Same hands/people as prior shots

**Colour grading:**
- Bright, clear, professional
- Ensure seal number is high contrast (dark text, light background)
- Warm tones (trust, care)
- No blur on seal (critical detail)

**Desktop crop (16:9):**
- Parcel and seal centred, hands frame it

**Mobile crop (9:16):**
- Vertical: Seal number prominent, fill 60% of frame
- Readable at 360px

**Purpose:**

**What's in the frame:**

- Courier or bus staff receiving parcel
- Hands in active handoff pose
- Background context (bus station, shipping area)

**What's NOT shown:**
- Driver's face or personal details
- Sensitive shipping info (full waybill number may be ok, but ask)

**Continuity markers:**
- SAME SEAL NUMBER and parcel from shot 3
- Same parcel size and wrap
- Sealed, untampered

**Colour grading:**
- Natural transit hub lighting (may be harsh, but authentic)
- Slight amber/sunset if possible (journey feeling)
- Ensure seal and waybill readable

**Desktop crop (16:9):**
- Handoff centred, parcel and waybill visible

**Mobile crop (9:16):**
- Vertical: Seal and waybill prominent
- Show motion/handoff energy

**What's NOT shown:**

**Colour grading:**

**Desktop crop (16:9):**
- Buyer centred, parcel and seal visible

## Lighting & Colour Grading (All Shots)

**Palette:**
- Primary: Warm green (#2D7F5E) — trust, Ghanaian landscape
- Accent: Deep amber (#C97F3A) — security, warmth
- Neutrals: Natural skin tones, market tones, home tones
- NOT: Cold, sterile, corporate (avoid blue casts)

**Mood:**
- Authentic, trustworthy, professional but warm
- Not dramatic or theatrical
- Daylight preferred (natural, approachable)
- Slight warm colour grade across all shots

**Mobile Usability:**
- All shots must be readable at 360px width
- Seal numbers, faces, key details must be legible
- Avoid thin lines or small text overlays

---

## Crew & Talent

**Talent Needed:**
1. **Buyer** (Accra): 1 person, mid-20s to 40s, warm demeanor, patient for 2–3 takes per scene
2. **Seller** (Kumasi): 1 person, owns or has access to an item (phone, electronics, small parcel-able good)
3. **Agent** (Kumasi): 1 professional MoMo agent or market worker, experienced handling transactions
4. **Courier/Bus staff** (optional): Already at transit hub, brief cameo

**Consent & Privacy:**
- Signed release for all talent (video + photo use in SeeFirst marketing)
- Ghana Card is blurred (never readable)
- Buyer and seller may be identifiable, but accept risk (they're customers, part of story)
- All contacts: +233 numbers, WhatsApp for coordination

## Shoot Logistics

**Timeline:**
- **Day 1 (Kumasi):** Shots 2, 3, 4 (market, counter, transit)
- **Day 2 (Accra):** Shots 1, 5 (home, receiving)

**Transport & Permits:**
- Kejetia Market: Inform MoMo agent association (courtesy)
- Bus station: Ask permission, minimal disruption
- Accra home: Pre-arranged with buyer

**Backup Plan:**
- If shots are blurry or seal number unreadable, reshoot same scene next day
- If parcel damaged in transit, print and seal new one with same number

## Deliverables

For each shot:
1. **High-res RAW** (.CR2, .NEF, or .DNG) — for future touch-up
2. **Full-res JPEG** (2000px width, 16-bit colour)
3. **Desktop crop** (1920x1080, 16:9)
4. **Mobile crop** (540x960, 9:16)
5. **Web-optimized** (AVIF/WebP, max 200KB)

**Total files:** 5 shots × 5 formats = 25 files
